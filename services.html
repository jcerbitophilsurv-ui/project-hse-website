Services

What we install, and what's included.

Every system is designed around your roof and your electric bill. Here's what that covers.

Residential Installation

Every system is sized to your household's actual consumption and your roof's capacity. A Civil Engineer checks the roof structure on every job. We handle the electrical permit, the utility application and the installation.

Trusted Partnerships

Monitoring & Maintenance

Your first year is fully covered — we track how your system is performing, clean the panels and check the equipment. After that we're still the people who know your system: call us any time it's running, for any question about how it's working.

Reliable Solutions

Battery & Backup Power

With a battery, your home keeps running when the grid goes down. You decide how much it covers — from the essentials to the whole house.

Meaningful Impact

Solar for business

Offices · Warehouses · Multi-building sites

We design and install for business as well as for homes. The engineering is the same. What changes is how the system is sized, and how the numbers are worked out.

A commercial system is sized on how your site actually draws power through the working day, not on a monthly bill. That is why there is no calculator for it here — the figure would be wrong. Tell us about your site and we will come and look at it.
